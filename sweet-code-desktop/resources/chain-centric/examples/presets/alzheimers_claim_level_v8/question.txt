Analyze competing hypotheses about Alzheimer's disease causation:

Hypothesis A (Amyloid-Beta Cascade): amyloid-beta plaque deposition or soluble amyloid-beta assemblies are the primary etiological driver.

Hypothesis B (Tau Propagation): tau tangle pathology and trans-synaptic tau spread are the proximate lethal mechanism driving cognitive decline.

Hypothesis C (Neuroinflammation / Immune Dysfunction): chronic microglial activation and immune dysregulation are the primary drivers of disease initiation and progression.

Questions:
1. Which hypothesis has the strongest causal evidence? Rank by evidence type, not by rhetorical plausibility.
2. Are the three hypotheses mutually exclusive, or do they occupy different causal layers?
3. Which specific evidence items become unreliable, scope-limited, or overgeneralized after cross-examination?
4. Design an experiment that would distinguish causal primacy among A, B, and C.
